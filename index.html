Research interests研究方向

My research is in harmonic analysis, focusing on Chang–Wilson–Wolff (CWW) theory and multiparameter square functions, Dunkl analysis, and two-weight inequalities.

我的研究领域是调和分析，主要关注 Chang–Wilson–Wolff（CWW）理论与多参数平方函数、Dunkl 分析以及双权不等式。
